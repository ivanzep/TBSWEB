Company

Founded to integrate all aspects of design + build at any scale.

The Brown Studio is passionate about architecture and interior design. We are devoted to achieving the highest design potential within every project we touch. Working throughout the Western United States and Mexico, our studio has created a style appreciated for its sensitive use of form, color, material, technology, space, light and landscape.

Our work has been published on ArchDaily.com, commercial television and international books, and was selected for Dwell Magazine's San Diego Home Tour.

As founder and principal, Lindsay Brown believes in the Design + Build approach, which lets the studio see its vision through the entire construction process as designer and general contractor. Rory Brown, co-founder and principal of Interior Design, ensures the smallest details and finishes marry effortlessly to the overall architectural work.

The principals

Lindsay Brown

Principal · Hospitality Developer · Design-Build Operator

For nearly 20 years, Lindsay has built an integrated practice spanning hospitality development, architecture, construction, modular manufacturing, sales and marketing, and property operations. As owner of The Brown Studio, Brown Bag Builders, Shop The Brown Studio and hospitality assets across Southern California and Lake Tahoe, he approaches development as developer, designer, general contractor, owner and operator.

Read the full profile

His experience includes more than 500 completed projects, from high-end oceanfront residences and multifamily communities to large subdivisions, branded short-term rentals, hotels and luxury resorts, plus master planning for major developments in Los Angeles and the Oceanside wave pool resort, and the SHFT hotel in Austin for Adrian Grenier and Peter Glatzer.

Palisades Tahoe. Developer, general contractor, modular manufacturer and sales and marketing lead for a 62-home branded short-term rental development — from entitlements through design, construction, marketing and final sale, with record-setting pricing per square foot and sales velocity.

Vail, Colorado. Currently delivering 12 luxury townhomes on the last undeveloped parcel in West Vail, spanning entitlement, architecture, interiors and construction management with ski valet and short-term rental management options.

Four Seasons Aspen. Led the design team for the Shadow Mountain project with land planners, hotel operators and branding agencies.

Coastal Southern California. The Brown Studio and Brown Bag Builders develop, design and build Swell Property's portfolio of luxury short-term rentals in Encinitas (4th Street, Hygiea, Burgundy, Jason Street), with an eight-unit project on Sanford Avenue under construction, and are entitling 12-home and 50-home hospitality-driven subdivisions.

Hotels. Recently acquired the Hilltop Hotel in South Oceanside (17 keys, redesign and rebranding underway), joining La Costa Hotel. Casetta Hospitality provides pre-opening and on-site management; Freewyld Foundry provides revenue-management insight from 30,000+ properties; Manna Atelier is planned to lead food, beverage and events.

Operations. For 15+ years Lindsay and his wife and business partner have owned Porchlight Property Management, managing branded short-term rentals in Lake Tahoe, Point Loma and Encinitas — and the Encinitas headquarters hosts dinners and art events with partners such as Outstanding in the Field.

Across these ventures, a vertically integrated approach to hyper-local, experiential hospitality — destinations rooted in their communities and designed around how guests experience, inhabit and remember a place.

Rory Brown

Interior Design · Co-Owner

Education: Environmental Studies, UC Santa Cruz

Rory leads the studio's interior design, defining an appropriate modern interior environment for each project. Her aesthetic comes from a family history in the textural, dramatic world of Hollywood theater and film.

Her team researches innovative yet deeply classic finishes and builds a spatial narrative of timeless spaces and details, iterating "until they are just right." Rory also founded SOTA, a state-of-the-art modern materials showroom and resource center, and is the authority on material fit, finish and longevity across the studio and its affiliated companies.

    Construction · Brown Bag Builders

    Building as a project partner.

    Since 2009, we have been building single-family custom homes and commercial developments, and more recently multi-family and subdivision residential projects. Construction and fabrication have been part of our design + build process since we started in our office workshop.

    We take great pride in finding creative ways to control cost, from budgeting through production of each construction component. Our projects are a thoughtful representation of each client's goals and material objectives.

    The Foundry · Pre-Fab Modular

    The most efficient way to build, delivered turn-key.

    A San Diego pre-fab modular company with a team dedicated to design, engineering and building the most efficient projects possible. We take any project type at any scale from idea to turn-key product, leveraging affiliates The Brown Studio and Brown Bag Builders.

    We believe modular is the most efficient method of construction available, delivering big results quickly and at lower cost. Our facility provides a controlled building environment for the highest quality standards, including delivery and assembly at the job site for a truly integrated Design / Pre-Fab / Build process.

    The team evaluates and optimizes projects for prefabrication, weighing the most cost-effective direction, providing early cost estimates and evaluating transport routes and logistics.
